A user uploads a photo of a room; Claude lists the objects in it, SAM 3 outlines them, and the user recolors any object realistically in the browser, fixing outlines with a brush where the AI missed. With an account, rooms and their colors are saved into designs (a design can hold several rooms) and found again on any device; uploads that are never saved are deleted after a day. The solution has two repositories: **DesignApp.Api** (.NET 10, Clean Architecture) and **DesignApp.Web** (React 19 single-page app).'),
